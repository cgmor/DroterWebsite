                      Prior to taking an airway bite take a Digital scan of the
                      upper and lower jaws without a bite.

                      1. Trial and Positioning: Try the device in the mouth.
                      Adjust forward for a Class III bite or backward for a Class
                      II bite. Once positioned, note the orientation.
                      2. Silicone Application: Fill the device with Parkell Blue-
                      Mousse super fast silicone. A second reline can be
                      performed if needed.


                      3. Jaw Movement Assessment: With the device affixed to
                      the upper teeth, have the patient move their jaw as far back
                      as possible. Mark the position of the lower incisor and the
                      midline with a pencil. Repeat with the jaw moved as far
                      forward as possible.
                      4. Measurement: Remove the device from the mouth and
                      measure the total anterior-posterior movement. Typically,
                      this is around 10-12 mm.
                      5. Target Position Marking: Mark a point halfway
                      between the most retruded and most protruded positions.
                      This is the target position for jaw posture. Typically around
                      50% of the total movement is a good place to start.


                      6. Patient Tapping: Instruct the patient to repeatedly tap
                      their teeth on the airway bite, guiding them to tap at the
                      midway forward mark.
                      7. Flowable Composite Application: Once you are
                      satisfied with the forward arcing position of the lower jaw,
                      have the patient rest on hold there. Verify it is in the area
                      you marked with the pencil. Apply flowable composite to
                      the airway bite in front of and behind the lower incisors.
                      Cure the composite. This will hold the jaw in the desired
                      forward position.


                      8. Digital Scan: With the jaw held in the forward position,
                      perform a digital scan of the bite. This will align the upper
                      and lower arch models for the dental lab to fabricate the
                      sleep device.
                      9. Alternative: Silicone Bite: If not using a digital
                      scanner, a silicone bite can be created by squirting silicone
                      into the back of the device.
